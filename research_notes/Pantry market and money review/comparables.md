# Comparable products to Pantry: pricing, model and scale indicators (as of 2026-10-08)

Conventions used throughout:
- **[Company]** = figure stated by the company or in its filing/store listing. **[Third-party]** = estimate or report by a named outsider. **[Computed]** = my arithmetic on cited figures (shown under Inferences only). **Not found** = searched, nothing reliable.
- "STALE" marks a figure older than 18 months (before roughly April 2025).
- App Store figures are the US storefront, read 2026-10-08, unless stated. App Store IAP lists show price points only; they do not label which plan each price belongs to, and they include legacy and promotional price points.
- App Store version-history dates often omit the year; where I inferred the year I say so.

## 1. Parsnip (the "Duolingo for cooking")

### Takeaway
Parsnip is still live and was shipping updates through June 2026, sells a subscription (App Store price points $6.99-$7.00/month and $24.00-$49.99/year), and is backed by Duolingo's co-founders, but its only public scale figures are small: 419 US App Store ratings and "over 80,000 downloads" on a third-party profile. No funding amount, revenue or subscriber count is public.

### Cited Findings
- App name "Parsnip: Learn to Cook", developer Seed & Stone, Inc; free with in-app purchases; 4.9 stars from **419 ratings** (US App Store, read 2026-10-08). [Company/store listing] — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/parsnip-learn-to-cook/id1592325213)
- IAP price points listed: monthly $6.99, monthly $7.00, annual $24.00, annual $49.99 (listing does not say which is current vs legacy). — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/parsnip-learn-to-cook/id1592325213)
- Latest version 3.8.1 dated "June 5" (year not shown; the history lists 2.5.0 on Dec 16, 2025, so the 3.x releases from April 17 to June 5 are 2026). Recent releases added a "Starter Path", recipe videos, dish favouriting, streak progress on the home screen. Product is described as short game-like lessons with stars and streaks, plus recipes ("dishes"). — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/parsnip-learn-to-cook/id1592325213)
- Third-party profile (yespress.io, profile last updated 2026-07-03): founded 2021 in New York; founders Andrew Mao (CEO) and Dan Sosa; "about 16 employees"; "over 80,000 downloads across iOS and Android"; 4.9 stars on both stores; "6 million content views in 8 weeks" (January 2024 update, STALE); free tier with limited daily learning plus a premium subscription; AI content engine rolled out October 2024 in which AI drafts about 90% of lesson material (STALE). The profile does not date the 80,000 downloads figure or the headcount. [Third-party, apparently compiled from company updates] — [yespress.io](https://yespress.io/parsnip.md)
- Seed round: the same profile says the seed closed February 2023, backed by Duolingo co-founders Luis von Ahn and Severin Hacker and other angels; no amount given. The company's own post (dated Nov 3, 2022, STALE) says "the co-founders of Duolingo backed us" and gives no amount, no user numbers and no pricing; it cites PR wins (5 viral Reddit threads, #1 on Hacker News, #1 education product of the week on Product Hunt). [Company] — [Parsnip Substack](https://parsnip.substack.com/p/seed-raise); [yespress.io](https://yespress.io/parsnip.md)
- Trade coverage "Parsnip Builds The Duolingo Of Home Cooking" dated March 23, 2022 (STALE); body is paywalled, no figures retrievable. — [HNGRY](https://www.hngry.tv/articles/parnsip-builds-the-duolingo-for-home-cooking/)

### Inferences
- Status: active but quiet. Four-plus years after launch, with Duolingo-founder backing and a claimed 16-person team, the visible audience is small (419 US iOS ratings; 80k cumulative downloads if the third-party figure is right). This is the closest direct comparable to Pantry's "learn to cook as a game" pitch and it is not evidence of a large market found; it is evidence the category is hard to grow.
- No update since June 2026 (about four months at the time of reading) after a burst of near-weekly releases in April to June 2026. Not enough to call it dormant.
- Parsnip's price points sit above Pantry's planned $3.99/month. Its $24.00/year point (about $2/month) may be a discount or legacy tier; which price is live could not be confirmed.

## 3. Cooking time-management games (the casual "cooking game" genre)

### Takeaway
The mainstream cooking-game genre is free-to-play, IAP-plus-ads, and operates at tens to hundreds of millions of downloads (Cooking Fever 100M+, Cooking Diary 61M+, Good Pizza Great Pizza 300M+), with a publisher like Nordcurrent at about EUR 89M annual revenue in 2022; it is a different business from a small premium/educational title.

### Cited Findings
- **Cooking Fever (Nordcurrent)**: released 2014, "downloaded more than 100 million times", 22 locations and 880 levels, monetises upgrades and new ingredients rather than pay-to-skip; Nordcurrent had 100+ employees and 50 published titles. - Cooking Fever US App Store (read 2026-10-08): free; 4.5 stars from **345K ratings**; IAP packs from $1.49 to $24.99 (e.g. Diner Pack $1.49, Bistro Pack $3.99, Buffet Pack $6.49, Cafeteria Pack $12.99, Restaurant Pack $24.99); listing declares advertising. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cooking-fever-restaurant-game/id714796093)

Whole-company figures, not Cooking Fever alone. Published 2023 (STALE). [Company via press] — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/nordcurrent-saw-a-42-increase-in-revenue-last-year-despite-a-turbulent-market)

- The genre's economics rest on volume, ads and consumable IAP, all of which Pantry's guardrails rule out (no ads, no timers, no lives). These titles show that "cooking" is a proven casual theme, not that a no-ads educational game has a comparable revenue path.

- Sensor Tower's "cooking games" bucket is topped by merge and even bingo titles, so genre revenue rankings overstate what cooking-mechanic games earn.

- Rating counts suggest order-of-magnitude audience tiers: NYT Cooking 548K, Paprika 54K, Kitchen Stories 23K, Crouton 2.9K, Mela 877, Parsnip 419. Rating counts are not downloads and accumulate over different lifetimes.

## 5. Duolingo as the model for gamified learning

### Takeaway
In Q2 2026 Duolingo reported 58.7M DAU, 140.6M MAU and 12.7M paid subscribers, and subscriptions were $258.0M of $298.5M revenue; advertising and in-app purchases together were under 10%.

### Cited Findings
- Q2 2026 (three months to June 30, 2026), global: DAU 58.7M (up 23% YoY from 47.7M); MAU 140.6M (up 10% from 128.3M); paid subscribers 12.7M at period end (up 17% from 10.9M). [Company filing] — [Duolingo 8-K shareholder letter, SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026053299/q2fy26duolingo6-30x26share.htm)
- Q2 2026 revenue: total $298.5M (up 18%); subscription $258.0M (up 22%); advertising $21.1M (up 2%); Duolingo English Test $10.1M (flat); in-app purchases $8.0M (down 23%); other $1.3M. Total bookings $289.1M (up 8%); subscription bookings $250.3M (up 10%). [Company filing] — [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026053299/q2fy26duolingo6-30x26share.htm)
- Guidance: Q3 2026 revenue $302M; FY2026 revenue $1,207M (16.3% YoY) and bookings $1,285M (10.9% YoY); FY adjusted EBITDA $320M (26.5% margin). [Company filing] — [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026053299/q2fy26duolingo6-30x26share.htm)

### Inferences
- [Computed] Paid subscribers as a share of MAU, Q2 2026: 12.7M / 140.6M = 9.0%. A year earlier: 10.9M / 128.3M = 8.5%.
- [Computed] DAU/MAU ratio, Q2 2026: 58.7 / 140.6 = 41.7%.
- [Computed] Q2 2026 revenue mix: subscription 86.4%, advertising 7.1%, Duolingo English Test 3.4%, in-app purchases 2.7%, other 0.4%.
- Duolingo's roughly 9% payer share is the outcome of more than a decade of scale, brand and a free tier funded partly by ads. It is a ceiling reference for a gamified learning app, not a planning assumption for a new one; the RevenueCat freemium median in section 7 (about 2% download-to-paid at day 35) is the more relevant base rate.
- Duolingo monetises by subscription, not by one-time course purchases. Pantry's $9.99 one-time cuisine track has no analogue in Duolingo's model.

## 6. Other games or apps that score a user-built dish or teach flavour pairing as a game

### Takeaway
The only close precedent found is Chef League (2019), a free-to-play iOS game by a Harvard professor that had players pick ingredients to fix a dish and judged them with an AI palate model; it has 27 ratings and has not been updated since February 2021. No active, commercially visible game that scores a user-built dish against a cuisine's logic was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Chef League**: multiplayer iOS game in which players pick ingredients to fix a dish that needs better flavour; "AI Chefs" judge based on historical palate preferences, with social voting when unclear; meant to teach flavour concepts. Built almost entirely by Beth Altringer, a Harvard engineering professor. Free-to-play; ingredients earned in play or bought in a shop. No download figures; the article says it did not yet have many players, with average sessions of 30 to 60 minutes and 3.5 sessions a day. Article May 31, 2019 (STALE). — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/chef-league-challenges-you-to-improvise-recipes-like-the-pros/)
- Chef League store tracker: free, 27 reviews averaging 5.0, last update Feb 23, 2021 (STALE); current availability unconfirmed. [Third-party tracker] — [AppFollow](https://apps.appfollow.io/ios/chef-league/1437820584?country=us)
- DishGuise (section 2) pairs an ingredient-deduction puzzle with a recipe at the end, but the player guesses a dish and does not build or get scored on one. — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/dishguise-a-game-of-culinary-deduction)
- Parsnip (section 1) teaches technique and ingredient knowledge through quiz-style lessons with stars and streaks; its listing does not describe scoring a dish the user composes. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/parsnip-learn-to-cook/id1592325213)
- A search for flavour-pairing and dish-scoring games also surfaced App Store entries named "Flavor Forge: Recipe Discovery" and "Spingredients"; neither was opened, so what they do is unverified. — [App Store: Flavor Forge](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6740825654); [App Store: Spingredients](https://apps.apple.com/app/id415115813)

### Inferences
- Pantry's core mechanic (compose a dish, get a deterministic score against a cuisine's grammar) appears to be unoccupied as of this search. That is consistent with either an open niche or a niche others tried and could not grow; Chef League, with academic backing and long sessions among early players, is evidence for the second reading at least as much as the first.
- Chef League's design differs in ways that matter: multiplayer, AI-judged and with purchasable ingredients. Pantry's single-player, deterministic, no-consumables design is not tested by Chef League's outcome.

### Gaps
- Chef League downloads, revenue, funding and reason for going quiet: not found.
- Flavor Forge and Spingredients: not examined.
- This is an absence-of-evidence finding from a handful of searches ("flavor pairing game app", "learn cooking game iOS", "score your dish"); a store-side keyword search on a device would be a stronger check.

## 7. Indie premium/edu iOS revenue: public disclosures and base rates

### Takeaway
The best available base rate is RevenueCat's 2026 dataset: the middle 50% of subscription apps earn $16 to $429 a month one year after launch, 17% of new apps reach $1k MRR within two years and 4.6% reach $10k; freemium apps convert about 2% of downloads to paid by day 35. No revenue disclosure from an indie recipe app or indie educational game developer was found.

### Cited Findings
- RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2026 (published March 2026; 115,000+ apps, $16B+ revenue; global unless noted): one year after launch, the middle 50% of apps earn **$16 to $429 monthly revenue**; top 10% earn $2,500+ (also cited as $2,574); mean about $72. [Third-party: RevenueCat, own platform data] — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026 insights](https://revenuecat.com/sosa-26-insights/)
- Share of new apps reaching milestones in their first two years: **$1k MRR 17%** (down from 19%); **$10k MRR 4.6%** (down from 5.3%). ### Inferences
- A realistic planning range for Pantry's subscription one year in, absent a marketing engine, is the RevenueCat middle band: tens to low hundreds of dollars a month, with roughly a one-in-six chance of passing $1k MRR within two years. Disclosed success stories (HabitKit) are top-few-percent outcomes and are in a utility category.
- [Computed, illustrative only] At the freemium median of 2.1% download-to-paid, 10,000 downloads would yield about 210 payers. Parsnip's reported 80,000 lifetime downloads at that rate would be about 1,700 payers ever. These are arithmetic on medians, not forecasts.
- RevenueCat's data covers subscription apps on its platform; it says nothing direct about one-time purchases such as a $9.99 cuisine track. No benchmark for one-time educational content packs on iOS was found.
- Low-priced subscriptions retain better (36% annual retention vs 23% for high-priced), which supports a low price point such as $3.99/month, but low-priced apps also show the lowest payer LTV ($10.69 median after a year).

### Gaps
- Revenue disclosures by solo developers of recipe apps (Mela, Pestle, Crouton, Paprika) or of indie educational/premium iOS games: not found.
- Benchmarks for one-time-purchase content packs or premium (paid up-front) iOS games: not found.
- RevenueCat median monthly and annual price points, and education-category figures: not on the pages read (the full report PDF was not opened).
